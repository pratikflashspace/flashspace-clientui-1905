    Get instant answers to the most common questions about our solutions and services.

    registration

    What is a virtual office and who needs it?

    A virtual office provides a premium business address without the high costs of physical office space. It is perfect for startups, freelancers, remote teams, and businesses expanding to new cities, offering benefits like GST registration, mail handling, and a professional image.

    pricing

    Are there any hidden charges?

    No, FlashSpace believes in transparent pricing. All costs are clearly mentioned upfront — what you see is what you pay. No surprises.

    pricing

    Why is your pricing higher than some competitors?

    We provide premium, legally compliant addresses at prime locations with end-to-end support including GST registration, mail handling, and dedicated account management.

    pricing

    Do you offer monthly and yearly plans?

    Yes, we offer both monthly and annual plans across all our services. Annual plans come with significant savings of up to 30%.

    pricing

    Can I upgrade my plan later?

    Absolutely. You can upgrade your plan at any time. The difference in pricing will be prorated for the remaining billing period.

    registration

    Is the virtual office address legally valid?

    Yes, all our virtual office addresses are legally valid and accepted for GST registration, company incorporation, and other regulatory requirements.

    registration

    Can I use this address for GST registration?

    Yes, our addresses are fully compliant for GST registration. We also provide the NOC and utility bills needed for the process.

    registration

    Will GST officers physically verify the address?

    GST officers may conduct physical verification. Our team ensures the premises are always ready and our staff is trained to handle such visits.

    registration

    Is this address accepted by banks?

    Yes, our virtual office addresses are accepted by major banks for current account opening and other banking formalities.

    documents

    What documents will I receive?

    You'll receive an agreement, NOC, utility bills, and a welcome kit. All documents are legally valid and ready for regulatory use.

    documents

    When will I receive the documents?

    Documents are typically delivered within 24–48 hours of completing KYC verification and payment.

    coworking

    What is the difference between Hot Desk and Dedicated Desk?

    A hot desk is a shared, first-come-first-served seat. A dedicated desk is your personal, reserved workspace available 24/7 with storage.

    coworking

    Are utilities included in coworking plans?

    Yes, all coworking plans include high-speed WiFi, electricity, AC, housekeeping, drinking water, and access to common areas.

    coworking

    Is there any lock-in period?

    No lock-in period for monthly plans. Annual plans require a minimum commitment but offer flexibility to upgrade or switch locations.

    coworking

    How do I book a meeting room?

    You can book meeting rooms instantly through the FlashSpace app or website. Select your city, preferred location, date, and time slot.

    How do I raise a support request?

    You can raise a support ticket through the FlashSpace app, email us at support@flashspace.ai, or call +91 8100888777. We're available 24/7.

    How long does support take to respond?

    Our average response time is under 2 hours. Critical issues are addressed within 30 minutes during business hours.

    support

    Can I cancel my booking?

    Yes, bookings can be cancelled as per our cancellation policy. Refund eligibility depends on the timing and type of service booked.
